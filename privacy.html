Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Purpose

This is a private application used by its owner to perform encrypted backups to Google Drive using rclone and Restic.

Google Drive Access

Google OAuth authorization allows rclone to access the owner's Google Drive account for storing and retrieving backup files.

Restic encrypts backup data before uploading it to Google Drive.

Data Sharing

This application is not offered to third parties. The owner does not sell or share personal data collected through this application.

Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. Backup retention is managed by the owner.

Revoking Access

Google account access can be revoked at Google Account Connections .

Contact

This application is maintained for personal use. Contact: GitHub profile .
